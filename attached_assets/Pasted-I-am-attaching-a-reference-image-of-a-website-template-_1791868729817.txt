LIVE WIB DIGITAL CLOCK
=====================
- Show the current time in Indonesia Western Time (WIB, UTC+7) as a digital clock in HH:MM:SS, 24-hour format.
- Calculate it in the browser with Intl.DateTimeFormat using timeZone "Asia/Jakarta", so it always shows WIB regardless of the visitor's device timezone. Update every second with setInterval. Never use the visitor's local time for this clock.
- Hero version: a small translucent glass pill in the bottom-left corner of the hero with a small green dot, the time in a tabular-number font (so digits do not jump), and the label "WIB · Indonesia (UTC+7)". Before JavaScript loads, show only the label, not a time.
- Contact page version: a rounded card next to the contact details with the title "My local time", the same live WIB clock, and the date (for example "Tue, 13 Oct"). Under it, a second row "Your local time" showing the visitor's own current time (use the visitor's resolved timezone) and the hour difference from WIB (for example "7 hours ahead of you" or "1 hour behind you"). Handle half-hour offsets correctly.
- Accessibility: do not announce the clock every second to screen readers. Give the clock a static aria-label "Current time in Indonesia, WIB" and mark the changing digits aria-hidden.
- Keep the clock code small, in its own script file, and reuse it on both pages.
